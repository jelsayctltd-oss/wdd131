Site Name

SkillBridge Learning Academy

SkillBridge Learning Academy is the name selected for this online learning platform. The name represents the connection between acquiring practical knowledge and achieving better educational and career opportunities.

Tagline: Practical Skills. Better Opportunities.

Site Purpose

The purpose of this website is to introduce SkillBridge Learning Academy and provide information about practical online training programmes in Microsoft Excel, Accounting and Finance.

The website will enable visitors to explore available courses, understand the learning opportunities offered and complete an online enrolment form. It will also communicate the academy's long-term vision of expanding into online secondary education.

Scenarios

The following scenarios represent questions that prospective students and visitors may ask when visiting SkillBridge Learning Academy.

  • What Microsoft Excel courses are available, and how can I enrol?
  • Can I learn practical accounting and bookkeeping online?
  • How do I register for a course at SkillBridge Learning Academy?
  • What learning opportunities are available for beginners?

Colour Schema

The website will use a professional colour scheme that communicates trust, learning, growth and innovation.

Primary Colour: Navy Blue

HEX Code: #102B46. Used for headings, the website header and primary navigation.

Accent Colour: Teal

HEX Code: #159B91. Used for buttons, links and important accents.

Background Colour: Light Grey

HEX Code: #F4F6F8. Used for page backgrounds and content sections.

Highlight Colour: Gold

HEX Code: #E9A23B. Used for highlights and special information.

Typography

Primary Font: Poppins

Poppins will be used for headings, titles and navigation because of its clean and modern appearance.

Secondary Font: Open Sans

Open Sans will be used for paragraphs, descriptions, forms and general website content because it is easy to read on different screen sizes.
